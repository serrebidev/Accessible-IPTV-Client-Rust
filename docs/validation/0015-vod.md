# Issue #38 (parity slice 8): VOD browsing + playback

Status: **automated gates PASS**. Implemented 2026-11-01 on branch
`agent/vod`. Python remains the shipping product and the behavior
oracle.

## Implemented behavior

- `iptv-core::vod` ports vod.py:
  - season/episode parsing for the three marker shapes (`S01E02`,
    `1x05` with digit boundaries, episode-only as season 0) and
    series-title stripping at the first marker;
  - episode ordering by (season, episode) with untagged items last in
    insertion order;
  - the M3U heuristic catalogue: only channels whose group-title
    explicitly hints movies/series qualify; a drill-in series folder
    requires 2+ episodes with 2+ distinct markers, everything else is
    a flat playable item; duplicate stream URLs collapse within a
    category; category labels are the group-titles verbatim;
  - the Xtream catalogue from the slice-5 client: movies with their
    category names under `Movies — <category>`, series lazily under
    `Series — <category>`; per-section failures skip just that
    section;
  - series episode expansion for both season shapes (keyed object and
    list), episode numbers from the row or position, labels
    `S01E02 - Title`, sorted on return;
  - catalogue merging preserving group order.
- A `VodWorker` builds catalogues off-thread (every Xtream account
  plus the loaded playlist's channels through the M3U heuristic — the
  browser worker gained an `all_channels` snapshot for this) and
  fetches episodes on demand.
- Adapter: a VOD button on the main window opens the VOD browser
  (groups list, items list, Play / Open, Back, its own spoken status);
  opening rebuilds the catalogue; movies play through the existing
  playback path (so resume, external players and the built-in player
  all apply); M3U series drill in inline, Xtream series fetch
  episodes in the background; closing the window hides it for reuse.
